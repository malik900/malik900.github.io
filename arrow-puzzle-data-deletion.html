Privacy Policy — Arrow Puzzle

Beatix Studio · Last updated 14 Nov 2026 · Applies to Arrow Puzzle (version 1.0.3 and later) for Android.

The short version

You can play without an account, a login, or giving us your name or email. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Unity Ads and uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played. We run no server that stores your game data, and there is nothing to buy in the app.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, settings and daily-challenge state are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes them.

What Unity Ads collects

Ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded) are served by Unity Ads. When an ad is shown, Unity may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information (model, OS version, language) and information about the ad shown and how you interact with it, so it can serve ads and measure them. Unity may share that advertising ID with its advertising partners. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy FAQ: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-faq.

You can opt out of personalised ads at any time: tap the Data Privacy icon on any Unity ad, or turn off ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization). You will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

What Firebase Analytics collects

The app uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to measure anonymous, aggregated gameplay — such as levels played and crashes — so we can fix bugs and improve the game. Firebase may collect device information, app version and approximate location (from IP address) for analytics. It does not collect your name, email or precise location. See Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

What we do not collect

We collect no name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or files. There is no account system, no in-app purchase and no shop, so there is no payment or purchase history.

Deleting your data

Because we hold no account and no server copy of your game data, there is nothing for us to delete from a server — clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device.

For data held by the ad and analytics providers, you can:

  • Ask Unity to delete the data tied to your device: email Unity's privacy team at dpo@unity3d.com, or use the Data Privacy icon on any Unity ad in the game. Include your device's advertising ID if you can.
  • Reset your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads), which breaks the link to past ad data.
  • Ask us anything about your data: email Beatix Studio at malikhasnainyasin900@gmail.com — include your advertising ID and what you want deleted, and we will forward the request to the provider that holds it.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children. We collect no personal data from anyone, and ads are served under the ad provider's own policies.
