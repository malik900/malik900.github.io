Privacy Policy — Document Scanner

Beatix Studio · Last updated 5 Nov 2026 · Applies to Document Scanner (version 1.6.5 and later) for Android.

The short version

Your scans never leave your device. We collect no personal data and show no ads. There is no account, no subscription and no cloud sync.

What stays on your device

Document images, PDFs and recognised text are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded. The app itself makes no network connections, and your scans, documents and their text are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

What Google's ML Kit libraries send

Scanning and text recognition are performed on the device by Google's ML Kit (document scanner 16.0.0, text recognition 16.0.1, barcode scanning 17.3.0). Those libraries send anonymous diagnostics to Google — device model, OS version, app version, and performance data such as latency and error codes — encrypted over HTTPS. They do not send your documents, images or recognised text, and Google states this data is not shared with third parties. The app has no control over, and receives no copy of, those diagnostics.

Permissions

Camera is used only when you scan a document or a code. No other permissions are requested. Files you open from other apps, and files you share out of the app, move only when you choose to do so, through Android's own chooser.

Listen (text-to-speech)

The Listen feature reads recognised text aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The app sends nothing itself; a cloud-based system voice engine, if that is what your device uses, may process the spoken text outside the app.

Deleting your data

Deleting a scan moves it to Trash for 30 days so you can restore it. After that — or straight away if you choose Delete forever — its files are permanently deleted from the phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
